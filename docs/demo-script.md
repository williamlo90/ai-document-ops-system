# AI-Powered Invoice Review & Approval System - Demo Script

Recorded artifact: [captioned MP4](assets/demo/invoice-review-demo.mp4).

## Story

1. **Inbox**: show invoices waiting for a decision and invoices blocked by a validation issue.
2. **Review**: compare the PDF with extracted fields and show that an unresolved blocker disables
   approval.
3. **Invoices**: show upload, lifecycle status, correction context, and deliberate document opening.
4. **Exports**: show approved-only eligibility, batch creation, and idempotent delivery controls.
5. **Quality**: show labeled synthetic results and the limitations stated in the UI.
6. **Operations**: show unresolved alerts, failed jobs, retry eligibility, service status, and audit.
